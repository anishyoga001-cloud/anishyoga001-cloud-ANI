What a retreat with Anitra holds

Every retreat is built from this set, paced to the group and the season.

Hatha & Yin Yoga

Slow, grounded practice — strength and stretch in equal measure, morning and evening.

Yoga Nidra

Guided deep-rest sessions for the nervous system, not just the body.

Sound Healing

Resonant instruments and silence, used to settle the mind after practice.

Meditation on the river stones

Sessions held at the water's edge, timed with the quiet hours of the day.

Naturopathy & Ayurveda

Traditional wellness practices woven through the stay, not bolted on.

Sattvic food

Simple, fresh, vegetarian meals cooked for how they make you feel afterward.

Rishikesh, where the river turns toward the plains

Rishikesh sits where the Ganga comes down from the Himalaya and slows for the first time — the reason it has drawn yogis, ashrams and pilgrims for generations.

Anitra's retreats are held close to the water, with evenings at the Ganga Aarti and mornings in the quiet before the town wakes.
